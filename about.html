About Us

Our History

Golden Crust Bakery began in 2015 when founder Sarah Nkosi started baking bread in her home oven and selling loaves to neighbours. Word spread quickly about the quality and taste, and within two years Sarah opened a small shopfront with the help of two part-time bakers. Today, Golden Crust Bakery is a beloved local institution known for traditional recipes, locally sourced flour, and zero artificial preservatives.

Our Mission

To bring warm, honest, handmade baked goods to every table in the neighbourhood, made fresh every single day.

Our Vision

To become the community's most trusted bakery, known for quality, consistency, and a personal touch that big chain stores cannot offer.
